# Attribution

Mondium is free software under the [GPL-3.0](LICENSE) by Gökdeniz (mondieuu).

Parts of the security hardening and several features were ported from [Calcium](https://github.com/cnrgks/Calcium), a GPL-3.0 browser derived from Mondium and maintained by cnrgks. This covers the verified CRX installer, bounded downloads, the local malicious-domain layer and warning page, user-script permission checks, the sandboxed UI preload, private-window filtering through uBlock Origin, deferred background tabs, the pinned tab grid, the task manager and downloads panels, the new tab box and the overlay scrollbar.

`vendor/secure-web-store.js`, `vendor/web-store-preload.js` and `vendor/ui-sandbox-preload.js` are generated by `tools/prepare-security.js` from electron-chrome-web-store and electron-chrome-extensions (Samuel Maddock, see [vendor/security-adapters.LICENSE.txt](vendor/security-adapters.LICENSE.txt)). uBlock Origin keeps its own license notices in `vendor/ublock`.
